Driving Lucy

Drag the stick the way you want her to go. Let go to stop. She switches to manual mode on her own the first time you move, and back again after 2 minutes without driving.

  • Up: forward. Slightly off up: gentle turn, further off: sharper turn.
  • Straight left/right: spin on the spot. Down: reverse.
  • Keys: WASD or arrows. W+A curves, A alone spins. Hold Shift for gentler turns. Space stop, P snapshot, F full screen.
  • Game controller: left stick drives, B stops, Y snapshot.

She has 8 fixed moves without root, so the stick snaps to the nearest one. The label at the top shows which.
