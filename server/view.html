Map


 

 

  
  
  
 

 
How the map works
  
Her live working map, straight from her memory. Tidy = cleaned up like the old app's map; Raw = everything she's seen,
   including stray sensor rays. Blue dot + line = where she thinks she is and which way she faces. Grid = 1 m. Dashed outlines = her rooms;
   red = keep-out zones; green = clean zones. Look does nothing on a tap. Go here: tap the map to send her there.
   Keep-out / Clean zone: tap corners (or drag a rectangle), then "Save to Lucy". Name room: tap inside its outline.
   Tick zones or rooms to clean just those (at the start she spends ~20 s working out where she is).

 
Saved maps
  

  

  

   
   
   
  

  
Taking her somewhere new: Save her map first (e.g. "home"), then New place, run her
   there and save that map too. Coming back: Load "home". Loading/blanking restarts her mapping program (~10 s) and keeps an
   automatic backup (grey "_before_" rows, newest 5 kept). Only while she's docked. The map guard puts her map back if a normal run
   saves a much smaller one.
